# Final Production Review

## 1. Review purpose
The final review exists to catch mistakes before export and ensure the video matches the brand, audience, and objective.

## 2. Review order
Check in this order:
1. script/story intent
2. audience match
3. visual continuity
4. text clarity
5. audio balance
6. effect and transition quality
7. CTA clarity
8. export correctness

## 3. Final script review
Ask:
- Does the message feel emotionally true?
- Does the video tell a clear story?
- Is the destination identity strong?
- Is the audience clearly addressed?
- Is the CTA placed after emotional payoff?

## 4. Final visual review
Check:
- each scene has purpose
- transitions are smooth and intentional
- pacing feels natural
- emotion escalates appropriately
- no weak visuals remain
- overall visual style is consistent

## 5. Final text review
Check:
- text is short and readable
- Russian wording is natural
- no text overload
- text timing matches visual rhythm
- CTA is visible and readable

## 6. Final audio review
Check:
- music matches mood
- volume is controlled
- SFX is intentional and not distracting
- voiceover is clear and timed well
- sound bed supports emotion

## 7. Final export review
Before export, verify:
- correct aspect ratio
- correct resolution
- correct format
- correct duration
- correct subtitles
- correct CTA frame
- file naming follows spec
- preview passes on the target platform

## 8. Final sign-off checklist
A video is ready when:
- brand tone is clear
- story is coherent
- emotional arc works
- audience desire is strong
- visual quality is consistent
- CTA is natural and effective
- export matches required spec

## 9. Final pass rule
If any one of these fails, revise before export:
- audience relevance
- emotional clarity
- CTA strength
- text readability
- sound quality
- platform fit
