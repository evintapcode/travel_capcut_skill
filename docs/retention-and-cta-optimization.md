# Retention and CTA Optimization

## 1. Hook rules
The first 2–5 seconds decide whether the viewer stays.

Best opening patterns:
- strong visual beauty
- emotional question
- cinematic reveal
- destination identity shot
- sensory surprise

Example hooks:
- Вьетнам — это не просто страна. Это ощущение.
- Где природа, романтика и комфорт встречаются в одном кадре?
- Этот отпуск запомнится не на неделю, а на всю жизнь.

## 2. Retention principles
Keep viewers engaged by using:
- clear visual progression
- emotional pacing
- scene changes with purpose
- short text blocks
- varied shot types
- music that supports mood

## 3. Retention beats
A strong travel ad should contain:
- curiosity
- wonder
- intimacy
- emotional pull
- reward
- action

These beats should appear in sequence, not randomly.

## 4. Scene duration guidance
- intro: 2–5s
- setup: 5–8s
- destination reveal: 5–12s
- experience scenes: 5–15s each
- emotional payoff: 5–10s
- CTA: final 3–5s

## 5. CTA optimization
CTA should be:
- short
- emotional
- easy to understand
- aligned with the final mood
- placed after the emotional payoff

Best CTA structures:
- destination + action
- feeling + action
- premium experience + action

Examples:
- Откройте Вьетнам вместе с нами
- Забронируйте отдых своей мечты
- Путешествие начинается здесь
- Ваше идеальное путешествие ждёт вас

## 6. Best practice for Russian-language CTA
Use clear, natural, elegant Russian.

Avoid:
- robotic jargon
- heavy English-brand phrasing
- awkward direct marketing language
- overly long CTA text on screen

Prefer:
- short but emotionally rich lines
- elegant, premium wording
- clear action verbs

## 7. Final frame strategy
The last frame should feel like a reward, not just text.

Use:
- scenic still or final montage
- big destination moment
- strong brand line
- CTA cleanly visible

## 8. Retention quality audit
Before final export, confirm:
- first frame is strong
- pacing feels intentional
- scenes are not repetitive
- stock music does not overpower
- emotional progression is clear
- CTA is placed at the right moment
