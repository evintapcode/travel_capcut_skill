# Production Checklist for Travel Video Content

## A. Pre-production
- Brief is complete
- target audience defined
- duration specified
- platform specified
- tone defined
- CTA defined
- brand identity reviewed
- references collected
- asset list prepared
- missing assets identified

## B. Content planning
- hook decided
- story arc built
- key scenes identified
- emotional beats mapped
- visual rhythm planned
- text length planned
- music mood selected
- sound design planned

## C. Asset review
- footage quality checked
- image quality checked
- licensing checked
- color continuity reviewed
- match between scenes verified
- crops and aspect ratio checked

## D. Story and pacing
- no scene is too long without a reason
- no scene is text-heavy
- every scene supports emotional progression
- scenes move from discovery to desire to action
- final CTA lands cleanly

## E. Editing quality checks
- audio levels consistent
- text readable on all screen types
- transitions smooth
- motion natural and purposeful
- color grade consistent
- final pacing feels intentional

## F. Final sign-off checklist
- audience fit confirmed
- brand tone aligned
- CTA correct
- subtitles readable
- music not distracting
- sound FX not excessive
- no obvious continuity issues
- export settings correct
