# Brand System for Travel Video Ads

## 1. Brand positioning
Define the emotional promise of the travel brand in one sentence.

Example:
- We turn Vietnam into a feeling people want to live.
- We help travelers experience the soul of Vietnam without rush.
- We design journeys that feel intimate, premium, and unforgettable.

## 2. Brand promise
What do customers expect from this brand?
- authenticity
- comfort
- beauty
- emotional depth
- memorable experiences
- trustworthiness

## 3. Brand values
List 3–5 values that every campaign must reflect.
- authenticity
- premium experience
- discovery
- emotional richness
- effortless travel

## 4. Brand voice
The brand voice should feel:
- warm
- premium
- evocative
- confident
- culturally aware
- emotionally immersive

Avoid:
- generic sales copy
- overuse of slogan stacking
- too much text on screen
- cold corporate tone

## 5. Emotional archetype
Choose one core emotional archetype:
- romantic
- adventurous
- luxury
- cultural
- family-oriented
- nature-first

## 6. Brand messaging hierarchy
Use this order:
1. destination emotion
2. experience value
3. trust / credibility
4. CTA

Example:
- Vietnam feels alive in every moment.
- Every stop tells a story.
- Travel with ease and elegance.
- Book your journey today.

## 7. Visual identity rules
- prefer warm cinematic colors
- keep contrast controlled
- use subtle grain and cinematic lighting
- lean into natural textures and real travel footage
- avoid over-edited or synthetic visuals when authenticity matters

## 8. Content system rules
Every output must answer:
- Why this destination?
- Why this trip?
- Why this audience?
- Why now?
- Why trust this brand?

## 9. CTA positioning
CTA should be placed after emotional payoff, not before it.

Strong CTA examples:
- Откройте Вьетнам вместе с нами
- Забронируйте отдых своей мечты
- Путешествие в Вьетнам начинается здесь

## 10. Brand review checklist
Before final export, verify:
- message matches brand values
- tone feels premium and authentic
- audience is clearly targeted
- emotion leads the story
- CTA lands at the right time
- visuals are not generic or overused
