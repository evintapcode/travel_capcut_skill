# CapCut Quality Control Guide

## 1. Frame quality
- check all footage for focus, stabilization, and exposure
- remove weak shots early
- avoid jittery footage unless intentional
- maintain visual consistency across scenes

## 2. Text quality
- avoid long lines of text
- use short, high-impact Russian lines
- ensure text contrast is strong enough on all backgrounds
- keep text readable in social viewing conditions
- align text to the visual rhythm

## 3. Audio quality
- normalize volume across scenes
- avoid muddy mixes
- emphasize atmosphere when needed
- keep music supportive, not dominant
- use sound effects sparingly and purposefully

## 4. Motion quality
- use camera motion intentionally
- slow drift is good for mood
- rapid motion should have emotional purpose
- avoid excessive transitions

## 5. Transition quality
Prefer:
- cross dissolve
- soft wipe
- match cut
- fade to black
- gentle slide

Avoid:
- overusing flashy transitions
- abrupt cut changes without emotional purpose

## 6. Color grading
- use a cinematic warm tone for travel mood
- keep skin tones natural if used
- maintain consistent white balance
- preserve atmosphere of the place

## 7. Composition rules
- lead with wide scenic shots
- use close-ups to create intimacy
- vary shot sizes to avoid monotony
- frame landscapes with a clear sense of place

## 8. Retention rules
Video should keep viewers engaged through:
- strong opening frame
- clear visual identity in first 2–5 seconds
- dynamic but not chaotic pacing
- emotional progression from wonder to desire
- clean end-frame CTA

## 9. CapCut export checklist
- resolution: 1080x1920
- format: MP4
- codec: H.264
- fps: 30
- subtitles checked
- audio normalized
- final render preview reviewed
- final CTA visible at end
